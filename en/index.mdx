Overthinker lets you define how an AI Agent responds, connect it to phone and web chat, and review the resulting conversations. Agents handle recurring inquiries and use tools for connected tasks. After a conversation, use its record and customer history to prepare the next response.

## What can you do?

- Answer customer questions using your instructions and business knowledge.
- Receive calls through connected numbers, place a single call, or run an outbound campaign.
- Add text-based web chat to your website.
- Call external APIs or Google app tools during a conversation to look up or record information.
- Review transcripts and analysis, and transfer configured SIP calls to a human agent.

## How does it work?

Follow **create an Agent → publish Live → connect a channel → review the conversation**.

Give the Agent a role, response guidelines, reference knowledge, and tools. During a phone conversation, speech recognition (STT) turns the customer's speech into text. The execution model (LLM) chooses a response or tool call, and speech synthesis (TTS) turns the reply into audio. Adjust the voice, interruptions, and silence behavior under [Voice profiles and conversation settings](/en/ai/voice).

Draft edits do not immediately affect customers. Use **Deploy** to select a Live version, then connect a phone number or web chat endpoint. New conversations use that publication. Web chat is currently text-only.

## Divide work between Agent nodes

An Agent can contain several nodes with different responsibilities. Choose the arrangement that fits your workflow in [Create and publish an Agent](/en/ai/agents).

- **Supervisor**: A coordinating node delegates work to specialist nodes and uses their results to continue the conversation.
- **Handoff**: Another node takes over the conversation when a condition matches. Use it when responsibility changes, such as moving from intake to detailed guidance.

A handoff between Agent nodes is different from a transfer to a person. For human assistance, check the [transfer tool and SIP call requirements](/en/operations/conversations).

## Key features

- **[Tools](/en/ai/tools)**: Connect REST APIs, external MCP and A2A services, and Google apps to an Agent's work.
- **[Knowledge search](/en/ai/knowledge)**: Upload documents and select a Revision for the Agent to consult.
- **[Channels](/en/operations/channels)**: Connect a number assigned to your account or a website chat endpoint to a Live Agent.
- **[Outbound campaigns](/en/operations/campaigns)**: Review a consent-backed recipient CSV and call recipients sequentially.
- **[Conversations and customers](/en/operations/conversations)**: Review transcripts, analysis, and customer timelines, and manage customer memory.
- **[Live conversations](/en/operations/monitoring)**: Follow the state, transcript, and events of active voice calls on your account.
