---
title: "Voice"
description: "Create a voice profile and adjust behavior for new voice sessions"
---

Choose a voice in **Voice profiles**. Sign in and open **Voice profiles** (`/dashboard/settings/voice-profiles`) in the app. An administrator must enable usable STT/TTS models and configure provider credentials before you can save a profile.

## Voice profiles

1. Select **Add voice profile** and enter a name. Under **Voice**, choose a TTS model and a compatible voice; play a preview.
2. Under **Speech recognition (STT)**, select a provider and model. Enter terms such as company names, one per line (up to 100 terms, 100 characters each).
3. If needed, adjust response speed, interruptions, noise cancellation, voice-activity sensitivity, background sound, DTMF input, and outbound dial wait. Select **Create profile**.
4. Check that the list shows the profile as **Available** with a revision. You can designate an available profile as the **default**. Direct browser voice sessions use the default when no profile is specified.

If the profile says **Needs attention** or cannot be saved, check that the speech models are enabled, provider credentials exist, and the voice is compatible with the TTS model. Ask an administrator to configure models or keys if you cannot do so.

## Conversation settings

There is no separate channel-management screen. Existing phone bindings and their voice profiles remain intact. Voice does not apply to current text web chat.

**Conversation settings** (`/dashboard/settings/conversation`) apply to the **account, not a specific Agent**. Choose privacy masking and whether to retain encrypted originals, an end-of-call message and end phrases, maximum call length and silence timeout, idle follow-up (fixed text or AI-generated, interval and count), and timed announcements or end actions. Select **Save settings**. Retaining encrypted originals is a separate choice from masking.

<Note>
When a **new voice session starts**, the profile revision, voice, STT/TTS models, and conversation settings are captured for that conversation. Changes to the profile or account do not modify an ongoing call. The maximum call length is capped by the shorter of the account setting and available time.
</Note>

Start a **new call** through the connected number to check the voice and end behavior. If there is no audio, first check that the channel's profile is **Available** and the Agent has a current Live deployment. You cannot delete the default profile, one assigned to a channel, or one used by an active session.

## Custom voices

Only when **Cartesia** is the TTS provider and its credential is configured, open **My voices** and upload a name and a WAV, MP3, or WEBM file (at most 10 MiB). Once the voice is **Available**, choose it under **Voice** in a Cartesia profile and save. The file extension must match its MIME type. A custom voice in use by a profile or active session cannot be deleted.
