---
title: "Monitoring"
description: "Check the status, transcript, and events of calls on your account"
---

Sign in and open **Live conversations** (`/monitoring`) to follow **voice calls**. This customer screen shows calls owned by your account, not calls across every account.

1. Choose **Active** or **Ended in 24 hours**. The recent list covers only the **last 24 hours**. Optionally filter by **SIP/WebRTC**, enter a search term, select **Search**, and use the page controls.
2. Review the active, pending, transferring, human, and recently failed counts, then select a call. Check connection status, elapsed time, current Agent nodes, latest tool, and the **Transcript / Events / Connection details** tabs.
3. Updates run automatically while the page is visible. Select **Pause** to stop automatic updates, then **Resume updates** or **Refresh monitor** to continue. A background tab does not poll.

<Note>
**Updates delayed** (stale) or **Execution status unavailable** means observation data is late or missing. **Showing the latest transcript entries/events** means earlier entries were truncated; this is not a complete call record or audit log. If a refresh fails, retry and check the connection state. This screen has **no audio listen or playback control**. To take part in a call, [join it from conversation details](/en/operations/conversations).
</Note>
