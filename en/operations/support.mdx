---
title: "Support and privacy"
description: "Send an inquiry, set protection for new conversations, or request account deletion"
---

You can send an inquiry from **Contact us** (`/contact`) without signing in. If you need to control which conversation data is protected, sign in and save your settings before starting a new conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send an inquiry">
    On **Contact us**, enter your name, phone number, and message. Agree to **personal information collection and use**, then select **Send inquiry**. Marketing consent is optional. The message **Your inquiry was submitted. We will be in touch soon.** and cleared fields confirm receipt; a team member will review it and contact you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set protection for new conversations">
    After signing in, open **Conversation settings → Privacy protection** (`/dashboard/settings/conversation`). Choose whether to **Mask personal information** and which categories to protect: name, phone number, date of birth, national ID, address, and email. When masking is on, select at least one category. Set **Keep encrypted originals** separately, select **Save settings**, and check for **Conversation settings saved.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The privacy policy is snapshotted when a new conversation starts; changing it does not rewrite earlier conversations. **Keep encrypted originals** controls whether protected original values are stored separately. It is not an option to reveal originals or remove masking on this screen.
</Note>

**Settings → Delete account** does not offer instant self-service deletion. To request deletion, send your account details and request through the inquiry form for support handling. The form is not a live chat or an immediate resolution channel. If submission fails, review your entries and required consent, then retry.
