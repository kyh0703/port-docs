---
title: "Account and billing"
description: "Review your profile, plan, balance, and purchase transactions; buy calling time only where payments are enabled"
---

Sign in and open **Settings** to check your account and calling time. The plan is read-only; prepaid calling-time purchases work only in environments where payments have been enabled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your profile">
    In **Settings → Profile**, edit your name or phone number and select **Save**. Check the values shown after saving. Email is read-only; passwords and social sign-in are managed by your identity provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your plan">
    In **Settings → Plan**, check the status, period, and allocated, used, and remaining calling minutes. If no plan has been assigned, the page says **You don't have an active subscription.** You cannot sign up for, change, or cancel a plan or set up recurring payments here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your balance and purchase records">
    In **Settings → Balance & usage**, check available and reserved time and the increases or decreases from purchases and use. Use the button for more transactions when available. **Settings → Invoices** lists only purchase transactions, showing their date, KRW amount, and completed status. If there are none, the page says **No invoices.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Invoices** is a purchase-transaction list, not a PDF download, receipt, or tax-invoice issuing feature. For plan requests or account issues, [contact support](/en/operations/support).
</Note>

## Calling-time purchases

Open **Settings → Balance & usage → Top up**, or **Payment method**. Enter a number of minutes within the displayed minimum, maximum, and increment. If you have a promotion code, enter it; select **Continue to payment**, review the discount and final KRW amount, then pay by **card**. After the success screen, check the balance and purchase transaction again.

Payments are disabled by default; check the actual purchase screen for availability. Seeing **Payments are currently unavailable.** means you cannot proceed. The confirmed prepaid-time usage path is WebRTC voice conversations. SIP outbound calling checks plan entitlements, so do not assume that prepaid balance covers every call.
